Bailey Bunny
on Acme Street!

A chaotic endless runner adventure!

Bailey Bunny decided to go out on a jog because he ate a lot of brownies and pizza! But as he was jogging all around town, he found Acme Street, where people just love to roll around bombs on the street without a care in the world.

Also, a bunch of money is lying on the ground; I suppose that people don't care about their wallets anymore in this town.

It's YOUR job to ensure that Bailey doesn't get blown up by any of the bombs on the road. If you do find money, like gold coins on the road, just take them; I'm sure no one will stop you.

Good luck!
